Denture

The presentation

82 year old female patient came to see us requesting a new upper denture.

Planning and options

After a meticulous treatment planning process, the patient decided to have the BEST possible denture without the use of dental implants. Every option was set out — including implant-retained alternatives — before the decision was made.

The outcome

A precision metal-based denture was constructed with our laboratory partners, restoring both appearance and chewing function while remaining entirely non-surgical.
