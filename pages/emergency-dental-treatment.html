General dentistry

Emergency dental treatment

We do our best to see our regular patients the same day.

If you have chipped or broken a tooth as a result of an accident or injury, it is very important that you see a dentist as soon as possible. The same goes for one or more knocked-out teeth.

Dental pain rarely improves on its own, and an injured tooth is often easier to save in the first hours than the following week. Call the practice as early in the day as you can and we will do our best to fit you in.

We do our very best to provide same-day emergency services for all of our regular patients, and will also try to accommodate others where we can. Members of Our Plan pay no emergency access fee and have worldwide dental injury and emergency cover.

What to expect

How it works

01

Call us first

Phone 01379 897176 rather than emailing. We can often give advice over the phone that helps straight away.

02

Knocked-out adult tooth

Hold it by the crown, not the root. If it is clean, try to put it back in the socket and bite gently on a clean cloth. If not, keep it in milk and come straight in.

03

Broken or chipped tooth

Keep any fragments. Rinse with warm salty water and avoid chewing on that side until you have been seen.

04

Swelling

Facial swelling that is spreading, or that affects your eye, breathing or swallowing, needs urgent medical attention — go to A&E.

05

Out of hours

When the practice is closed, NHS 111 can direct you to the nearest urgent dental service.
