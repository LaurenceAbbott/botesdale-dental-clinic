We are your trusted local family dental practice for all kinds of general dentistry.

General dentistry

General dentistry is the everyday care that keeps a mouth working: diagnosing what is wrong, fixing it properly, and keeping it that way.

It covers examinations, fillings, root canal treatment, extractions and the emergency care you hope never to need. Most of it is unglamorous, and all of it matters more than any cosmetic treatment we offer.

We see whole families here — children, parents and grandparents — often on the same afternoon. Whatever brings you in, you will be told what we find, what your options are and what each one costs, before anything is agreed.

The process

How treatment works here

01

Examination

A full assessment of teeth, gums and soft tissues, with radiographs where they are needed.

02

Diagnosis in plain English

You are shown what we have found — on screen where that helps — and what it means.

03

Options and costs

Every reasonable option is set out with its cost, including doing nothing for now where that is safe.

04

Treatment

Carried out at a pace that suits you, in as few visits as the work sensibly allows.

05

Review and prevention

We agree a recall interval based on your individual risk, not a blanket six months.
