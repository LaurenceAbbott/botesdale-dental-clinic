Case study

Same day teeth

Same day teeth

The presentation

The patient came to see us with 6 teeth remaining in the lower jaw. Until this moment the patient could manage. Unfortunately the bridge was beyond repair and the patient faced some tough decisions.

Planning and options

Every option was discussed in full, including a conventional lower denture. The patient chose an implant-supported solution allowing a fixed set of teeth to be delivered without a prolonged period without teeth.

The outcome

The result restored a full lower arch with a fixed, implant-supported prosthesis, and with it the ability to eat and speak with confidence.
